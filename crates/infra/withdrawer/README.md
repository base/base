# Withdrawer configuration and signing

`base-withdrawer` provides `WithdrawerArgs`, a clap parser, and `WithdrawerConfig`, its typed configuration. Conversion validates settings without deriving keys or accessing devices. This library does not contact networks, build proofs, or submit transactions.

Supply `--l1-rpc-url`, `--withdrawal`, and exactly one signing source. `--private-key` accepts a 32-byte hex key, also available through `WITHDRAWER_PRIVATE_KEY`. `--mnemonic` accepts an English BIP-39 phrase as one quoted value or separate words, also available through `WITHDRAWER_MNEMONIC`. Conversion joins the words with single spaces and validates the phrase. `--ledger` is available only with the `ledger` Cargo feature. Mnemonic and Ledger sources use `--hd-path`, defaulting to `m/44'/60'/0'/0/0`. Paths must have at most 255 components. Each component must have an index between 0 and 2147483647, with or without a hardened suffix. Secrets have redacted debug output and parse errors. Prefer environment variables over argv to avoid exposing secrets in process listings.

`WithdrawalSigner::new(&SignerSource)` asynchronously creates a local signer or connects to a Ledger. Raw keys must be nonzero secp256k1 scalars. Mnemonics use standard BIP-39 with an empty passphrase and BIP-32 at the configured path. The signer implements `alloy_network::TxSigner<Signature>` for its address and transaction signing; `is_ledger()` identifies hardware signers. Debug output never includes signing secrets.

The optional `ledger` Cargo feature enables `alloy-signer-ledger` and the Ledger signing source. Connect and unlock the device and open its Ethereum app before construction. Ledger paths must have at most 10 components, the Ethereum app's limit. Ledger signatures must recover to the device's address. Legacy transactions are refused on chain IDs where the device's truncated EIP-155 parity cannot be decoded reliably, such as 111. Use EIP-1559 fees on those chains instead.

`--network` selects `base-mainnet` by default, or `base-sepolia`, `op-mainnet`, or `op-sepolia`. Each preset uses fault proofs. For a custom network, omit `--network` and supply `--l2-rpc-url`, `--portal`, and either both `--dispute-game-factory` and `--anchor-state-registry`, or `--l2-output-oracle`. Addresses and withdrawal hashes require exact-length hex values. Custom contract addresses must not be zero.

Fees default to automatic estimation. `--gas-price` selects legacy fees. EIP-1559 requires both `--max-fee-per-gas` and `--max-priority-fee-per-gas`. Fees are decimal wei and must fit `u128`. `--max-gas-price` optionally caps explicit fees and later automatic estimation. `--gas-limit` overrides estimation and conflicts with `--gas-multiplier`, whose default is 1.0 and which must be finite and at least 1.0.

`--timeout` defaults to `10m`, `--max-retries` to 3, and `--retry-delay` to `2s`. Durations use humantime syntax and must be positive. At most 32 retries are allowed, and conversion rejects retry delays whose exponential backoff would overflow a Rust duration. `--dry-run` disables signing and submission in later layers.
